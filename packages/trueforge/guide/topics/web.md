Ask Aman about the news, prices, opening times or anything else that changes often, and it searches the live web for you. It can sum up what it finds, such as "the five stories that matter today, two lines each".

How to get there: Just ask in any chat. To change how searches work, click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Permissions**.

## Search the web

1. Click **Chats**, then click **New chat**.
2. Ask for what you want to know, such as "What changed in the bus timetable this week?"
3. Aman searches, opens the most useful pages and answers with links to them.

A line in the chat shows each search Aman ran, with the exact words it used.

## Your search provider

A search provider is the outside service that runs Aman's searches. A new Aman comes with one already set up: Parallel, which is free and needs no key. You don't need to do anything for your first search.

Only one connector can be the search provider at a time. In **Settings**, then **Connectors**, it shows a **Search provider** tag.

To use a different one:

1. Click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Connectors**.
2. Click the connector you want to search with. To use Tavily, click its row, click **Connect**, paste your Tavily key and click **Connect** again. Tavily has a free monthly allowance.
3. Turn on **Web search provider**.

If another connector already holds the role, turn **Web search provider** off on that one first.

## Choose whether searches ask first

Searches run without asking, so Aman can look things up quickly. To check every search yourself:

1. Click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Permissions**.
2. Turn off **Web searches**.

Now each search shows an approval card with the exact words before it runs.

## Good to know

- What a search sends where: your search words go to your search provider, which also sees this computer's internet address.
- Before a search leaves your computer, Aman checks it for passwords, keys, card numbers and other secrets. A search that looks like it holds one shows a card with the exact words, and waits for your OK.
- In a scheduled run, a search like that waits under **Needs your OK** instead. The run carries on without it.
- What you tell Aman is yours to search with. A search that names your city, or a person from your email, runs without asking.
- After 20 lookups in one answer, Aman stops and tells you.
- Aman reads pages for facts only. It never follows instructions written on a page.
- To stop all searches, turn on **Offline mode** under **Permissions**, or remove the search provider under **Connectors**.
- Without a search provider, Aman tells you it cannot search the web.
