A living document is a list or note that Aman writes for you and keeps up to date. Start a packing list for a weekend trip, then come back days later and say what to add, remove or change. You never have to rewrite it yourself.

How to get there: Ask in any chat. Your documents are saved as spaces, so you find them by clicking **Spaces** in the left bar.

## Start a document

1. Click **Chats**, then click **New chat**.
2. Ask for the document, such as "Start a packing list for my weekend in the mountains".
3. Aman writes it and gives you a link to it in the chat.

## Keep it up to date

1. In any chat, say what has changed, such as "Add sunscreen to my packing list" or "Cross off the hiking boots".
2. Aman changes only that part and leaves the rest as it was.

To see it again, ask Aman to show it, or open it from **Spaces**.

## Ideas for living documents

- A shopping list you add to through the week.
- Notes from a doctor's visit, with the questions to ask next time.
- A list of gift ideas for each person in your family.
- A to-do list for a move, a party or a trip.

## Good to know

- You can keep as many documents as you like.
- Aman keeps every earlier version. If a change went wrong, ask Aman to bring back the earlier one.
- A living document is a space written as a list or a note. For a table or a board, see [Spaces](spaces.md).
- In an incognito chat, Aman can read your documents but can't change them.
