title: Finding forgotten subscriptions
covers: subscriptions
---

Ask Aman to look through your email for subscriptions and renewals. It lists what you pay for, how much, and how often, and it flags anything you may have forgotten.

Aman only reads your mail for this. It does not cancel anything. If you want to cancel something, Aman can help you write the message, and you approve it before it is sent.

This needs Gmail connected. See "Connecting your email and calendar".
