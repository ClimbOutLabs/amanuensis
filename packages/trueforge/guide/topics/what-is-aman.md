Aman is your personal assistant. It helps you keep track of your day, your email, your calendar, your tasks and the things you want to remember.

You talk to Aman in plain words, the way you would talk to a person. Start with a simple question or a task, and Aman asks for anything else it needs.

## What stays on your computer

Aman runs on your own computer, not in the cloud. Your chats, your files and what Aman remembers stay there.

Only two things leave your computer:

- What you send to the AI model you choose. The model is the part that writes Aman's answers, and it runs at the company you picked, such as OpenAI or Anthropic.
- The words of a web search. They go to Parallel, the search service Aman uses unless you choose another one. Parallel also sees your internet address.

Aman checks with you before it does anything that matters. Reading is free. Sending, saving or changing something needs your OK first.

## What Aman is not

- Aman is not a website you sign up for. You install it once, and it runs on your computer.
- Aman does not come with an AI model. You connect one from a company you choose, and that company bills you for what you use.
- Aman does not act behind your back. It shows you what it wants to do, and waits for your answer.

## What you need

- A Mac, a Windows PC, or a Linux computer with Ubuntu or Debian. It needs 16 GB of memory and 20 to 45 GB of free disk space.
- An API key for an AI model, from Together AI, OpenAI, Anthropic or Google Gemini. An API key is a long password that lets Aman use your account at that company.
