Aman is your personal assistant. It keeps track of your life and helps you with it: your day, your email, your calendar, your tasks, and the things you want to remember.

Aman runs on your own computer. Your chats and your notes stay there.

Aman checks with you before it does anything that matters. Reading is free. Sending, saving or changing something needs your OK first.

Aman is a set of helpers, and you can ask for any of them in plain words. Start with a simple question or a task, and Aman will ask what it needs.
