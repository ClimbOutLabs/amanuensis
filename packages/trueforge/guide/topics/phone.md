title: Using Aman from your phone
covers:
---

You can use Aman from your phone when the phone is on the same home network as your computer.

The first time, open the "Trust this computer" link that the installer printed, or scan the square code on the screen. The page shows the steps for your phone. You do this once for each device. After that, open the address that ends in 8443 from the list the installer printed.

If the page says the connection is not private, the phone does not trust your computer yet. Do the trust steps again on the phone.
