A goal is an outcome you ask Aman to keep track of over time, such as getting a community fundraiser ready. Aman checks the sources you pick, records what it finds as evidence against the goal's steps, and shows you what is next.

How to get there: Choose **Goals** in the main navigation. You can also ask in a chat, for example "Make this a goal", and Aman makes the draft for you.

## Make a goal

To make a goal, do the following:

1. On the Goals page, choose **New goal**. In a chat, ask Aman to make the goal instead.
2. Say the outcome in one sentence, and how you will know it is done.
3. Add the steps the work breaks into, in order. A step can wait on an earlier step.
4. Pick the sources Aman may read for this goal, such as one document. A check reads only the sources you picked for that goal, and nothing else.
5. Pick the agent that will do the checking.

The goal is saved as a draft. A draft is never checked, and nothing runs until you activate it. You can pick the agent on the draft, or change the one you picked, any time before you activate it.

## Activate a goal

Before a goal starts, Aman shows you an activation card. The card names the agent setup that will do the work, with its model, instructions, connections, and skills, and the limits on each check.

To activate a goal, do the following:

1. Open the goal and read its activation card.
2. Choose **Activate**.

Activation pins the setup on the card to the goal. If the agent's setup changes afterwards, the next check is held and the card is shown again with the new setup, so you always know exactly what is doing the work. Nothing about signing in to a connection is ever pinned. The card names connections only.

## Check a goal now

To check a goal now, do the following:

1. Open the goal.
2. Choose **Check now**.

Aman reads the goal's sources, saves anything new as evidence on the step it belongs to, and updates the steps. The goal page shows the evidence, what changed, and the next step. A source Aman could not read is named on the page, so a quiet goal is never mistaken for a checked one.

Note: A draft cannot be checked. Activate the goal first.

## Pause or end a goal

To pause a goal, open it and choose **Pause**. A paused goal is not checked, and approvals already waiting for it no longer stand. Choose **Resume** to start again.

To end a goal, open it and choose **End**. An ended goal keeps its history, and it is not checked again.
