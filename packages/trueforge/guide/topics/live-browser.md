The live browser shows you the page Aman has open in its own browser, so you can do a step yourself, such as finishing a sign-in or passing a human check, in the same tab Aman is using.

Aman sends you to the live browser when a page needs a step only you can do. What you do there stays in the tab, and Aman carries on from the same page when you go back to the chat.

## To use the page yourself

1. Open the live browser link Aman gives you in the chat.
2. Click the picture of the page, scroll over it, and type, just as you would on the page itself.
3. When you are finished, click **Done**, then go back to the chat and tell Aman you are done.

## To take over the page

Some human checks refuse the live browser's ordinary clicks, because a click there arrives all at once, with no hand behind it. Takeover sends your own pointer movements, presses and releases to the page instead.

1. Click **Take over** above the picture.
2. Move and press on the picture to use the page, just as your hand does.
3. When you are finished, click **Hand back**.

While you hold the tab, Aman sends nothing to it. Aman starts again once you hand the tab back.

Note: While you hold the tab, a line above the picture says when the site is asking for a human check. The live browser spots many human checks, but not all of them, so no line does not always mean there is no check.

Note: If you leave the live browser without clicking **Hand back**, the tab is handed back for you. Aman is never left waiting on a tab nobody is driving.
