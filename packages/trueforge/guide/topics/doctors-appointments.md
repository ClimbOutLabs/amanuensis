Ask Aman to help with your doctor's appointments. It looks for appointments in your calendar and your email, tells you what to bring and what to ask, and reminds you of anything you still need to book.

To find appointments in your calendar and email, connect both first. See "Connecting your email and calendar". Without them, tell Aman the details and it will keep track for you in the chat.
