Ask Aman to find a place, such as a quiet coffee shop near you, and to plan the walk there.

Aman uses a map service to look up places and routes. It sends the place names and routes to that service, and only after you approve the card. Each place shows only the details its web page states, with a link to the page. A detail the page does not give is left out, not guessed.

To find places, pick a web search provider in Settings first. See "Searching the web".
