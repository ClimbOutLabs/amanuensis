Aman can find places for you, show them on a map and plan how to get there. Ask for a quiet coffee shop near your office, a doctor who takes new patients, or the bus that gets you to a meeting on time.

How to get there: Just ask in any chat. In a new chat you can also start from the **Find places and routes** idea card. See [Ideas to try](ideas.md).

## Find places

1. Click **Chats**, then click **New chat**.
2. Say what you are looking for and where, such as "Find me an English-speaking doctor near Central Station".
3. Aman searches the web, reads the two or three best pages and pins up to three places on a map card.

Each place on the card shows only what its web page states: name, address, languages, hours, price and phone. It also says which site the details came from, with a link. A detail the page doesn't give is left out, never guessed.

A place that is already in your calendar is marked **Already in your calendar**.

## Plan a route

1. In any chat, name where you start and where you are going, such as "How do I get from Central Station to the city library by bike?"
2. Aman draws the route on a map card, with the distance and about how long it takes.
3. For public transport, the card lists each step: which line, where to change and where to get off.

You can travel by walking, cycling, driving or public transport. If you don't say, Aman walks short trips in town and takes public transport for longer ones.

## Find out when to leave

For public transport, say when you need to be there, such as "I need to be at the library at 9:20". Aman finds a connection that arrives in time and tells you when to leave. You can also say when you want to leave instead.

## Good to know

- Name both places. Aman doesn't know where you are right now.
- Finding places needs a web search provider. Aman comes with one already set up. See [Search the web](web.md).
- Maps run without asking. To check each one first, click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Permissions**, and turn off **Maps**. A map that looks like it would send a password or key always asks.
- With the **Maps** switch off, each map shows a card first. **Always allow in this chat** stops the question for that chat. **Always allow everywhere** turns the **Maps** switch back on.
- With the **Maps** switch off, a scheduled run, such as your morning brief, skips its maps.
- What a map sends where: the place names you look up go to free map services built on OpenStreetMap. Public transport trips go to Transitous, a free timetable service. The map pictures come through Aman, so those services never see your browser.
- For a route, the route service gets your starting point rounded to about 100 meters.
- Maps don't work while **Offline mode** is on.
