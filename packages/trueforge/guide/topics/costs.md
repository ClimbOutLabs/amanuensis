Aman itself is free. What can cost money is the model: the company behind a hosted model may charge for the words each chat uses. A model that runs on your own computer has no charge per use.

How to get there: Ask Aman in any chat, or turn on **Agent details** at the top of a chat to see the cost of each answer.

## Ask Aman what you've spent

1. Open a chat.
2. Ask, for example, "Show me what my chats have cost so far this month."
3. Aman lists what your chats used, by model, with an estimated cost.

You can ask about today, this week, this month or any range of days. Deleted chats still count. An incognito chat counts too, but it's never named.

## See the cost of one answer

1. In a chat, turn on **Agent details** at the top of the chat.
2. Look at the details under an answer. A quiet line names the model, the words it used (counted in tokens, small pieces of words), and the cost.

The cost includes the helper jobs behind the answer, such as summaries for your memory.

To hide this line, click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Appearance**. Under **Answers**, turn off **Show model and cost in answer details**. Aman still counts your use. You just don't see it.

## Good to know

- Costs are estimates from the model's list price. Your bill comes from the model's company, not from Aman.
- If the model's company doesn't report how much a chat used, the line says "usage not reported".
- If you use Token Factory models, **Settings**, then **Models** shows a **Token Factory today** box. It shows today's use per model and how much of the daily limit is left. The count starts again at midnight UTC, which is the same time worldwide.
- An answer that failed or that you stopped shows no cost line, but its use still counts.
