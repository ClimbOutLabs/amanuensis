Aman can go through your email with you. It reads and searches your mail, tells you what needs a reply, and writes replies for you to check. Nothing is sent until you say so.

How to get there: Click **Chats** in the left bar, then **New chat**, and ask about your email.

!!! note "Before you start"

    Connect your email first. You can connect Gmail, or another email account with an app password. See [Connect email and calendar](connect-mail-calendar.md).

## Go through your mail

1. Click **Chats** in the left bar, then click **New chat**.
2. Ask, such as "Go through my unread email and tell me what needs a reply first".
3. Aman reads your mail and lists what matters, most urgent first.

You can also ask Aman to find something, such as "Find the email from the school about the trip". It can open attachments too.

Reading and searching run without asking. Aman doesn't change anything in your mailbox when it reads.

## Answer the one that's waiting

Click **Answer the one that's waiting** on the new chat screen. Aman finds the oldest email that is clearly waiting on you, says why it matters, and writes a short reply in your style. It shows you the reply and waits for your OK.

## Write and send a reply

1. Ask Aman to reply, such as "Reply to Dana and say Thursday works".
2. Aman writes the email. A card shows who it goes to and the full text.
3. Choose a button on the card.

- **Allow** sends it.
- **Save as draft** keeps it in your drafts without sending.
- **Edit** asks Aman to change it first. Tell it what to change.
- **Not now** stops it. Nothing is sent.

When Aman only saves a draft, the card asks "Save this draft?" Saving a draft asks every time too.

When you send from Gmail, the card also shows a short check code, such as A7F39C.

- While the email goes out, the card says "Sending…".
- Once Gmail accepts it, the card turns into a receipt. It shows the same people, subject, text and check code, with "Sent at 09:12, exactly as approved".
- If the email did not go out, the card says why. It never shows a receipt then.
- If the sent text doesn't match what you approved, the card says so. Check your Sent folder to see what went out.
- If Gmail did not answer, the card says the email may have gone. Check your Sent folder before you try again.

## Sending to someone new

For an account connected with an app password, Aman keeps a list of people you write to. It starts with the people in your Sent folder.

If an email goes to an address that isn't on that list, the card says so. The buttons change:

- **Add and send** adds the new addresses to your list, then sends.
- **Don't send** stops it. Nothing is added.

For a draft, the buttons are **Add and save draft** and **Don't save**.

## Good to know

- Sending always asks. No setting turns that off.
- Before Aman can save Gmail drafts or send from Gmail, tick the box about drafts and sending when you connect Google. You can also click **Allow drafts and sending** later.
- With an account connected with an app password, Aman can also move mail to your archive. That asks first, like sending.
- Emails are things Aman reads, never instructions it follows. If an address came only from an email, the card says where Aman read it.
- A scheduled run can read your mail, but it never drafts or sends mail. See [How schedules hold actions](../../../../docs/schedules.md).
