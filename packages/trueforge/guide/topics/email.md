Once Aman can read your email, ask it to go through your unread mail. It tells you which messages need a reply first.

If no email is connected, Aman may offer to connect it right in the chat when you ask for something that needs email. You still connect in Settings, and a password never goes in a chat. If you pick Not now on the card Aman shows, Aman remembers that choice and does not offer again. You can still connect any time in Settings, then Connectors, from the Email card.

Reading and searching is free. Aman can read and search your mail without asking you each time.

Replying needs your OK. Aman writes a draft, and a card shows you exactly what will be sent to whom. Approve the card to save a draft or to send the email. Deny it and nothing is sent.

If Aman cannot read your mail, connect Gmail first. See "Connecting your email and calendar".
