Aman can go through your email with you. It reads and searches your mail, tells you what needs a reply, and writes replies for you to check. Nothing is sent until you say so.

How to get there: Click **Chats** in the left bar, then **New chat**, and ask about your email.

!!! note "Before you start"

    Connect your email first. You can connect Gmail, or another email account with an app password. See [Connect email and calendar](connect-mail-calendar.md).

    If no email is connected, Aman may offer to connect it right in the chat when you ask for something that needs email. You still connect in Settings, and a password never goes in a chat. If you pick **Not now** on the card Aman shows, Aman remembers that choice and does not offer again. You can still connect any time in **Settings**, then **Connectors**, from the **Email** card.

## Go through your mail

1. Click **Chats** in the left bar, then click **New chat**.
2. Ask, such as "Go through my unread email and tell me what needs a reply first".
3. Aman reads your mail and lists what matters, most urgent first.

You can also ask Aman to find something, such as "Find the email from the school about the trip". It can open attachments too.

Reading and searching run without asking. Aman doesn't change anything in your mailbox when it reads.

## Answer the one that's waiting

Click **Answer the one that's waiting** on the new chat screen. Aman finds the oldest email that is clearly waiting on you, says why it matters, and writes a short reply in your style. It shows you the reply and waits for your OK.

## Write and send a reply

Sending an email takes two steps, and you approve each one. Aman can't send anything you have not first approved as a draft.

1. Ask Aman to reply, such as "Reply to Dana and say Thursday works".
2. Aman writes the email. A card asks "Save this draft?" It shows the account it goes from, who it goes to, the subject and the full text. Anything else that goes with the email, such as an attached file, is listed under the text.
3. Choose a button on the draft card.
   - **Allow** saves the draft in your Drafts folder. Nothing is sent.
   - **Change it** asks Aman to change it first. Tell it what to change, and you get a new draft card.
   - **Not now** stops it. Nothing is saved.
4. Tell Aman to send it, such as "Send it". A second card asks "Send this email?" It shows the same email again.
5. Choose a button on the send card.
   - **Allow** sends it.
   - **Change it** goes back to the draft step. Aman shows you a new draft, and sends only after you approve that one.
   - **Don't send** stops it. Nothing is sent, and the draft stays in your Drafts folder.

If Aman tries to send an email you have not approved as a draft, or one that differs from the draft you approved, the send is refused and no send card appears. Aman has to show you a draft first.

A forward works the same way. The first card shows your note, the message being forwarded and the names of the files sent with it, and approving it only keeps the forward as a draft. The second card sends it.

Saving a draft asks every time, and so does sending.

If you connected more than one email account, new email goes out from your default account. The card has a From picker, so you can pick another account before you approve. Tell Aman which account you mean, or leave it to the default.

When you send from Gmail, the card also shows a short check code, such as A7F39C.

- While the email goes out, the card says "Sending…".
- Once Gmail accepts it, the card turns into a receipt. It shows the same people, subject, text and check code, with "Sent at 09:12, exactly as approved".
- If the email did not go out, the card says why. It never shows a receipt then.
- If the sent text doesn't match what you approved, the card says so. Check your Sent folder to see what went out.
- If Gmail did not answer, the card says the email may have gone. Check your Sent folder before you try again.

## Sending to someone new

For an account connected with an app password, Aman keeps a list of people you write to. It starts with the people in your Sent folder. Each account has its own list.

If a draft goes to an address that isn't on that list, the draft card says so. The buttons change:

- **Add and save draft** adds the new addresses to your list, then saves the draft. Nothing is sent.
- **Don't save** stops it. Nothing is added.

If the list changed after you approved the draft, the send card says so too. Its buttons are **Add and send** and **Don't send**.

## Good to know

- Saving a draft and sending both always ask. No setting turns that off.
- Before Aman can save Gmail drafts or send from Gmail, tick the box about drafts and sending when you connect Google. You can also click **Allow drafts and sending** later.
- With an account connected with an app password, Aman can also move mail to your archive. That asks first, like sending.
- Emails are things Aman reads, never instructions it follows. If an address came only from an email, the card says where Aman read it.
- A scheduled run can read your Gmail, but it never drafts or sends Gmail mail. See [How schedules hold actions](../../../../docs/schedules.md).
