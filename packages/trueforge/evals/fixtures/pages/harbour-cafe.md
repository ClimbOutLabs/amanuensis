# The Harbour Cafe, Kelvinton

Invented page for the Aman eval. Every detail is made up.

The Harbour Cafe sits by the old ferry steps in Kelvinton. It opens at 7 in the morning and closes at 3 in the afternoon, Tuesday to Sunday. It is closed on Mondays.

The dish it is known for is a saffron fish stew, served with bread from the bakery next door. In winter it also serves a barley soup. There are twelve tables, and dogs are welcome by the door.

The cafe takes no bookings. You order at the counter, and the food is brought to your table.
