## Features

These are the same pages Aman's **Help, I'm stuck** answers from.

**Chat with Aman**

- [Chats](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/chats.md): Start, find, rename and organize your chats.

- [Files and pictures](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/files.md): Add a file or a picture to a message.

**Plan your day**

- [Your morning brief](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/daily-brief.md): One page with your day, your mail and what needs you.

- [Search the web](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/web.md): Get current news, prices and facts.

- [Aman's browser](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/live-browser.md): Watch Aman's browser, or take over to sign in.

- [Memory](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/memory.md): What Aman remembers about you, and how to change it.

- [Personas](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/personas.md): Helpers with their own name and model.
- [Ready-made personalities](personalities/README.md): Five personalities to start from.
- [Agents](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/agents.md): Saved instructions that do a job, and run your schedules.
- [Skills](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/skills.md): Teach Aman a new skill.
- [Appearance](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/appearance.md): Change Aman's colors and theme.

**Connect your accounts**

- [Connect email and calendar](../packages/trueforge/guide/topics/connect-mail-calendar.md): Pick the right card and connect your accounts.
