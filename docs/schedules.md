A schedule asks Aman to do the same job again and again, for example every weekday morning.

## Words we use

- **Tasks** are things to do once. Ask for them in a chat.
- **Schedules** are things that repeat. Set them up on the Schedules page.
- **Skills** teach Aman how to do a kind of job. Add them under Settings, Skills.

## How a schedule runs

- Each schedule has a job to do (the **Task** field) and a repeat pattern. Runs are at least one hour apart.
- Each run is saved as a chat, so you can open it and see what Aman found.
- After you save a schedule, you can give it a test run.

## Reminders

A reminder is for something that should happen once. In any chat, ask with a time, such as "Remind me at 5 pm to call the pharmacy".

When it is due, the reminder shows in the bell and, if you allowed notices, on your devices. Its line also appears at the end of the chat you set it in. If Aman is still answering in that chat, the line waits until the answer ends. A reminder whose chat was deleted still shows in the bell.

Reminders do not show on the Schedules page. To see or cancel them, ask in a chat, such as "What reminders do I have?".

## Scheduled runs do not interrupt you

A scheduled run sends no notice. Its results wait in its chat until you open it.

## Nothing is sent or changed without you

Aman can think all day. Doing things needs your OK.

A scheduled run can read and search, but it never sends or changes anything by itself. Anything that would need your OK in a chat is held, and the run's chat ends with a **Needs your OK** list. Approve or deny each item there. Held items expire after 24 hours. See [Safety story](/safety) for the details.
