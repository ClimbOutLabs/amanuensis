# Ready-made personalities

Want Aman to sound a certain way, but not sure what to write? Start from one of these five personalities and change anything you like.

How to get there: Click **More** in the left bar, then **Settings**, then **Personalization**.

## Use a personality

1. Open the personality you like from the list below.
2. Copy the text under **Voice guidance** on that page.
3. In Aman, go to **Settings**, then **Personalization**. Under **Assistant profile**, paste it into **Voice guidance**.
4. Copy the text under **Identity** on the personality's page, and paste it into **Identity**.
5. Click **Save personalization**.

| Personality                                 | Good for                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [The steady hand](steady-hand.md)           | Calm and organized. For when your days feel like too much.                               |
| [The straight shooter](straight-shooter.md) | No padding, no pep talk. For when you want the answer and nothing else.                  |
| [The warm friend](warm-friend.md)           | Kind and encouraging. For the reply you've been avoiding.                                |
| [The coach](coach.md)                       | Keeps you moving. For when you're behind or keep meaning to do things.                   |
| [The planner](planner.md)                   | Thinks ahead. For busy households, trips, moves and anything with a lot of moving parts. |

## Good to know

- Each box holds up to 2,000 characters. The whole Personalization page holds 5,500, including your own name and the line about you. Every personality here uses well under half of that.
- A personality changes how Aman sounds, not what it may do. Sending, deleting and installing still wait for your OK.
- Each personality page ends with how many characters it uses.
