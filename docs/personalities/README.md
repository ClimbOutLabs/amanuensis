# Ready-made personalities

Each file is a personality you can give your chief of staff. Open **Settings, Personalization**, find **Assistant profile**, and copy the two fields into **Voice guidance** and **Identity**. Change anything you like.

Each field holds up to 2,000 characters, and the whole Personalization page holds 5,500, including your own name and the line about you. Every personality here uses well under half of that.

None of them change what the assistant is allowed to do. Sending, deleting and installing still wait for your OK.

| Personality                                 | Good for                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [The steady hand](steady-hand.md)           | Calm and organised. For when your days feel like too much.                               |
| [The straight shooter](straight-shooter.md) | No padding, no pep talk. For when you want the answer and nothing else.                  |
| [The warm friend](warm-friend.md)           | Kind and encouraging. For the reply you've been avoiding.                                |
| [The coach](coach.md)                       | Keeps you moving. For when you're behind or keep meaning to do things.                   |
| [The planner](planner.md)                   | Thinks ahead. For busy households, trips, moves and anything with a lot of moving parts. |
